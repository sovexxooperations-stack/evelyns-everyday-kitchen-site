LUNCH & DINNER · 1 SERVING · 45 MIN

Lemon Herb Chicken with Carrots

You’ll need

  • 150 g raw chicken breast
  • 150 g carrot
  • 180 g potato
  • 10 g olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • ⅛ tsp black pepper

Kit: oven and thermometer

Method

  1. Heat oven to 400 °F / 200 °C. Scrub potatoes and cut into 2 cm chunks; slice carrots.
  2. Toss vegetables with half the oil, thyme and pepper in a roasting dish. Roast 15 minutes.
  3. Coat chicken with remaining oil and lemon; place beside vegetables. Roast 20–25 minutes more, until chicken reaches 165 °F / 74 °C at the thickest part and vegetables are tender.
  4. Rest chicken 3 minutes before slicing.

Evelyn’s tip: use a dish with space around the vegetables so they roast instead of steam.
