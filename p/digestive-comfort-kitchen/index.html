GOOD TO KNOW

A few useful answers.

Is this a physical book?

No. Your purchase is a digital download: two PDFs of the same cookbook, one for your phone or tablet and one formatted for US Letter printing. No physical book is shipped.

What exactly is included?

One cookbook with 40 distinct recipes, in two layouts. Each recipe has a defined serving, ingredients in US measures and grams, numbered steps, estimated nutrition, relevant swaps, allergens, and storage notes. Other cookbooks and the Kitchen Companion are not included.

How do I receive my files?

After a successful Payhip checkout, your download is available on the confirmation page. Payhip also emails your download link. Save the PDFs to your device to use them offline.

Are the recipes for one or two people?

Single-serving recipes show amounts for one and two people. Small-batch recipes show the total yield and exactly how many pieces or portions make one serving. Read the stated yield before preparing a recipe.

Do I need special equipment or ingredients?

The collections use familiar grocery-store ingredients and common kitchen equipment. Each recipe lists what you need. Ingredient availability and individual tolerance vary.

Is this a subscription?

No. Each cookbook is a separate one-time purchase of $11.99 USD, plus any applicable tax shown at checkout. There are no recurring charges.

Can I use this as a personalized diet?

These recipes provide general food ideas, not an individualized diet or treatment. Nutrition and times are estimates. The recipes were developed and editorially reviewed with research, rather than physically kitchen-tested.

What if I need help with my download?

Contact evelyn.brooks.contact@gmail.com with your order details. Review the purchase and refund terms shown by the seller before completing checkout.
