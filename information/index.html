Using the recipes

Recipes provide general food ideas. They are not personalized nutrition advice or medical treatment, and no specific health or weight result is promised. Read serving sizes, allergens, cooking temperatures and storage notes, and check ingredients and labels against your own needs. Nutrition values, where given, are estimates. Recipes have not been physically kitchen-tested; cooking times and yields are estimates.
